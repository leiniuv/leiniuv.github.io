About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Hong Kong Catastrophic Risk Centre (HKCRC) at Lingnan University. I completed my Dr. Phil. in the Cologne Center for Contemporary Epistemology and the Kantian Tradition (CONCEPT) at the University of Cologne. Before that, I studied philosophy at Humboldt University of Berlin and Southwest University of Political Science and Law.
