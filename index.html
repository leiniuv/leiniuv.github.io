Lei Niu

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Hong Kong Catastrophic Risk Centre (HKCRC) at Lingnan University. I completed my Dr. Phil. in the Cologne Center for Contemporary Epistemology and the Kantian Tradition (CONCEPT) at the University of Cologne. Before that, I studied philosophy at Humboldt University of Berlin and Southwest University of Political Science and Law. My research lies in social epistemology, applied ethics, and their intersection with artificial intelligence.

Research Interests)

  • Social Epistemology
  • Applied Ethics
  • AI
